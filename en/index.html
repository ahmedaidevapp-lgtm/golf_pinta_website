The academy

Learning golf the right way.

Led by Mustapha Pinta, professional coach and player, the academy teaches adults and children in Casablanca, Marrakech and Rabat.

Our certified coaches offer private lessons, group lessons and clinics for every level, with a modern, friendly approach. Our mission: to make golf accessible, fun and rewarding for everyone.
