Getting started in Casablanca

Learn golf
in Casablanca.

Whether you have never held a club or are coming back after years away, the Golf with Pinta academy welcomes you at The Tony Jacklin Casablanca, in Bouskoura, with lessons suited to your age and level.

Golf lessons for adult beginners

The easiest way to start is a group lesson: a pack of eight sessions for 1,500 MAD, or a single session at 250 MAD if you'd rather try it first, with no commitment. Slots are announced in the academy's WhatsApp group, which you can join without any obligation. Practice equipment is provided, so there is nothing to buy. If you would rather progress one-to-one with a coach, private lessons start at 200 MAD for 25 minutes, as single lessons or in packs of 10 — see all prices.

Golf school for children, from age 6

Juniors follow a year-long programme across four levels — Discovery, Mastery, Performance and Elite — with two sessions a week in groups of six to eight children. Each stage is signed off with coloured flags, tracked in the young golfer's logbook. A year starts at 4,200 MAD, academy polo shirts and cap included.

How to sign up

Message us on WhatsApp at +212 665 602 209 or fill in the booking form. Tell us the player's age, experience and availability, and we will point you to the right lesson and time slot, subject to availability.

Where lessons take place

At The Tony Jacklin Casablanca — Prestigia Golf, in Bouskoura (27182), south of Casablanca on the edge of the Bouskoura forest. The course has 18 holes, par 72, and tee times run every day from 7:00 to 18:30.

Frequently asked questions

Your questions,
answered.

How much does a golf lesson cost in Casablanca?

A 55-minute private lesson costs 300 MAD with a team coach and 400 MAD with Mustapha Pinta; 25-minute lessons start at 200 MAD. Groups share the price: a 55-minute lesson for four players is 600 MAD, or 150 MAD each. Juniors pay reduced rates; for adults, a group lesson is 250 MAD on its own, or 1,500 MAD for a pack of eight. Full price list.

What age can you start golf?

The golf school takes children from age 6, with clubs and targets sized for them. For adults there is no age to start: the group lessons are designed for people taking up golf or coming back to it.

Do I need to know how to play before signing up?

No, every level is welcome. The adult group lessons and the juniors' Discovery level are built for complete beginners. Experienced players join the Performance and Elite levels, or take private lessons to work on something specific.

Do I need my own golf clubs to start?

No. In the adult group lessons, practice equipment is provided, so there is nothing to buy to get started. For other lessons, just tell us when you book if you don't have clubs.

When do lessons take place?

Adult group-lesson slots are announced in the WhatsApp group as each course opens. Juniors train on Wednesday and Saturday afternoons, with Saturday and Sunday morning slots for some levels — see the 2026-2027 schedule. Private lessons are scheduled around the coach's availability.

Can I cancel a lesson?

Yes: a lesson cancelled more than 48 hours before its start time is refunded in full.

Where is the Casablanca golf academy?

At The Tony Jacklin Casablanca — Prestigia Golf, in Bouskoura (27182), south of Casablanca. Our coaches also teach in Rabat, on the Montgomerie course, and in Marrakech, on the Tony Jacklin and Montgomerie courses.
